# ICOR for Life - Scratchpad 0.3.0

The slim release: the floating window and the shortcut you record, and
nothing else.

- **Floating window**, on or off. Off means no system-wide shortcut and no
  window.
- **The shortcut opens** today's daily note (the default) or a new scratch
  note beside it, named from the minute. Two small buttons in the window
  switch between them, and three commands do the same from the palette.
- Scratch notes stay where they are. Nothing marks them processed and
  nothing moves them.

Removed: the menu bar icon, the vault ownership record and every file
outside your vault, the `obsidian://` link, the actions palette, the browse
list and the in-window chords. A 0.2 `data.json` keeps your shortcut,
always on top and the window position.

Needs Obsidian 1.13.0 or newer. Copy `main.js`, `manifest.json` and
`styles.css` into `<vault>/.obsidian/plugins/icor-for-life-scratchpad/`.
